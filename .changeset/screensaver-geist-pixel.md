Screensaver font option: the clock, date, track title and battery
percentage on the screensaver can now use Geist Pixel in any of its five
styles (Square, Grid, Circle, Triangle, Line) instead of Geist. Pick it
in Settings > Screensaver on the panel (each choice is drawn in its own
font), in the web settings and iOS app, or from Home Assistant's new
"Screensaver font" select. Geist stays the default. The Matrix rain
keeps its film glyphs.
