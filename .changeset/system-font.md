System-wide font: one setting now picks the typeface for the whole panel
(screensaver included) and the web settings. Choose Geist (the default),
one of Geist Pixel's five styles (Square, Grid, Circle, Triangle, Line),
any of Google Fonts' ~1,900 families, or upload your own .ttf/.otf from
the web settings. Google families download onto the speaker in the
background and are kept across updates; uploads can be removed again.
Pick built-ins and installed fonts on the panel (Settings > Font, each
tile drawn in its own font), search Google Fonts and upload in the web
settings (Display > Font), use the iOS app's Font picker and search, or
set the new Home Assistant "Font" text entity. The Matrix rain keeps its
film glyphs.
