Seven more emoji styles in Display > Emoji style: Samsung One UI,
Microsoft Fluent 3D, Huawei, WhatsApp, Facebook, JoyPixels, and Toss
Face. Like Apple's set, each one downloads straight from its pinned,
sha256-verified upstream source onto the speaker when you ask for it.

Twemoji moves to a Unicode 17 COLRv1 build - newer emoji, crisp at
every size. Speakers with the old Twemoji download drop it at boot and
fall back to Noto until Twemoji is downloaded again.
